Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 5, 2026

This app is used only by the owner of the Instagram account @masculine_philosopher to publish and manage its own posts.

What we collect

We do not collect, sell or share personal data from anyone. The app only accesses the @masculine_philosopher account it was authorized for, to publish posts and read basic account information.

Data from Meta

Access tokens from Meta are stored securely and used only to publish content to the authorized account. They are never shared with third parties.

Data deletion

To remove the app's access, go to Instagram or Facebook Settings, then Apps and Websites, and remove the app. You can also contact us and we will delete any stored data within 30 days.
